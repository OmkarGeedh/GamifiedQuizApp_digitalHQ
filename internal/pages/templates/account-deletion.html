Delete your SkillVerse account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open SkillVerse and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password. Your account is deleted at once.

Without the app

{{if .SupportEmail}}Email {{.SupportEmail}} from the address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. We confirm and delete it within 30 days.{{else}}Write to the contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing, from the address you signed up with, and ask for your account to be deleted. We confirm and delete it within 30 days.{{end}}

What is deleted

Your account, profile, progress, streaks, coins, gems, power-ups, rewards, purchase history and app events. A record of purchase checks is kept without anything that identifies you, for fraud prevention and accounting.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Quiz Pass subscription. Cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions so you are not charged again.
