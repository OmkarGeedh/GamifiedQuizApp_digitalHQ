SkillVerse privacy policy

Last updated: {{.Updated}}. This policy explains what the SkillVerse quiz app collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, display name and password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Profile choices: the class, board, subjects and interests you pick.
  • Learning and game activity: your answers, scores, streaks, rewards, and your coin, gem and power-up balances with their history.
  • Purchases: when you buy through Google Play we receive the product, the order number and a purchase token from Google, so we can confirm the purchase and add it to your account. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Rewarded ads: when you choose to watch an ad, Google AdMob shows it and confirms to us that it was watched, so we can give you the reward. We do not let ads be personalised to you. Google may process device and usage data, such as your IP address, to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • App events: app opens, reminder settings and leaving a game early. These help us improve the app; they contain no names or emails.

How we use it

To run the app and keep your progress, confirm purchases and rewards, prevent cheating, fraud and abuse, keep the service secure, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Children and teenagers

Until a user's age is confirmed, we treat everyone as under 18. Ads are never personalised, are limited to content suitable for general audiences, and appear only when you choose to watch one, never during a game.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the app: Google (Google Play for purchases, AdMob for the ads you choose to watch), and our hosting and database providers, under their own security and privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, progress, balances, purchases history and app events are deleted. A record of purchase checks is kept without anything that identifies you, for fraud prevention and accounting.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and data at any time in the app: Profile › Delete account. Other ways to request deletion.
  • Change your ad privacy choices in the app (Shop › Gems & Pass) where your region requires it.
  • Cancel a Quiz Pass subscription in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as hashes. Purchases are confirmed with Google before anything is added to your account.
